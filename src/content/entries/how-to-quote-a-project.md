---
title: How to quote a design project
situation: A client asks "what will this cost?" and you are not sure how to answer.
category: pricing
tone: neutral
author: Teja
script: |
  Thanks for the details. For [scope summary] my estimate is [price], covering [deliverables] and [number] rounds of revisions, delivered in [timeline]. Extra rounds are billed at [rate]. Happy to adjust the scope if you want to work to a budget.
---

## Why it works

A quote tied to deliverables, revisions and a timeline is easier to defend than a single number.
