---
title: Responding to "make it pop"
situation: Feedback is vague and you cannot act on it.
category: feedback
tone: friendly
author: Teja
script: |
  Thanks for the feedback. To make sure I change the right thing, could you tell me what you want people to notice first on this screen? Is the goal more attention, more clarity, or a different feel? That helps me choose the right fix.
---

## Why it works

It turns a taste comment into a goal, which is something you can design against.
