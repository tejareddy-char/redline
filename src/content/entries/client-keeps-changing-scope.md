---
title: Client keeps adding "just one more thing"
situation: The project scope has grown beyond what was agreed and you need to reset without souring the relationship.
category: clients
tone: firm
author: Teja
script: |
  Hi [Name], thanks for the new ideas, they're good ones. Those additions go beyond what we scoped in [agreement/date]. I can include them as a short add-on for [price] and [days] extra, or we can keep the current scope and queue them for phase two. Which do you prefer?
---

## Why it works

It praises the idea, names the agreement, and offers two clear choices, so the client picks a path instead of arguing about fairness.
